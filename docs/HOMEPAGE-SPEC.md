# Homepage Structural Specification — Freyer Logistics
**Document Version**: 2.0  
**Principle**: Specification of layout, evidence requirements, and interaction hierarchy (No final copy).

---

## Section 1: Global Mega-Menu Header & Top Utility Bar
- **Elements**: Official Freyer Navy/Red Logo, Service dropdown preview, 10-City Location selector, emergency phone trigger, high-contrast **"Request a Quote"** primary CTA button.
- **Accessibility**: Keyboard navigable with ARIA expanded states and escape-key dismissal.

## Section 2: Flagship Hero (Multimodal Logistics Hub)
- **Visual Composition**: High-speed compressed WebM/MP4 video montage of container vessels, air freighters, and heavy-lift machinery hauling.
- **Headline Architecture**: Headline stating core multimodal capability; Subheadline defining Pan-India and global reach.
- **Embedded Floating Card**: 3-Tab Quick Trigger: **Quick RFQ / Find Nearest Branch / Track Status [Future]**.

## Section 3: Verified Trust & Accreditation Ribbon
- **Visual Composition**: Clean contrast bar displaying verified badges: **AEO Certified**, **IATA Agent**, **WCA World**, **SCN Partner**, **Great Place to Work**.
- **Evidence Requirement**: Strictly verified badges only; zero unverified industry claims.

## Section 4: Core Logistics Capabilities Matrix
- **Visual Composition**: 6 interactive capability cards (Air, Ocean, Customs, Warehouse, Project Cargo, Risk Management) with real photography.
- **Interaction**: Subtle hover elevation; instant deep-link to individual service specs and quote wizard.

## Section 5: Heavy-Lift Project Cargo Differentiator
- **Visual Composition**: Editorial showcase of real over-dimensional cargo operations (Transformers, turbines, plant machinery).
- **Evidence Requirement**: Authentic project photos with verified technical descriptors.

## Section 6: Pan-India Branch Network Overview
- **Visual Composition**: Clean interactive India map highlighting the 10 verified hubs (Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Vizag, Coimbatore, Tuticorin, Ahmedabad).
- **Interaction**: Selecting a city reveals direct branch manager phone, email, and address.

## Section 7: Guided Multi-Step RFQ Callout Banner
- **Visual Composition**: High-contrast brand red/navy banner inviting shippers to configure a customized freight quotation.

## Section 8: Global Comprehensive Footer
- **Visual Composition**: Full sitemap navigation, regulatory disclosures, registered office (Bengaluru) and operational hub (Chennai) notes, legal links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Carriage), dynamic current-year copyright.
