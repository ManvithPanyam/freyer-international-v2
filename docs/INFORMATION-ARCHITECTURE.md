# Information Architecture Specification — Freyer Logistics

---

## 1. Verified Route Structure & Sitemap

```
[Freyer Modern Information Architecture]
├── / (Home — B2B Flagship Conversion & Operational Proof Hub)
├── /about (Corporate Overview, 2018 Legacy, Leadership & Accreditations)
├── /services (Services Hub with Interactive Capability Matrix)
│   ├── /services/air-freight (Air Charters, Express, Cold-Chain, Dangerous Goods)
│   ├── /services/ocean-freight (FCL, LCL, Breakbulk, Reefer & Port Operations)
│   ├── /services/customs-brokerage (AEO-Certified CHA, ICEGATE, Tariff Advisory)
│   ├── /services/warehousing (Secure Storage, Inventory Management, 3PL Distribution)
│   ├── /services/project-cargo (Heavy-Lift Engineering, Multi-Axle, Over-Dimensional Cargo)
│   └── /services/risk-management (Marine Transit Insurance & Loss Prevention)
├── /projects (Filterable Technical Case Study Showcase)
├── /locations (Interactive Directory of 10 Pan-India Hubs & Port Proximity)
├── /careers (Culture, Great Place to Work Certified, Job Openings & Resume Drop)
├── /contact (Departmental Inquiry Routing: Sales, Operations, Billing, General)
└── /quote (Dedicated 5-Step Guided Freight RFQ Engine)
```

---

## 2. Route Purpose & Conversion Mapping

| Route | User Purpose | Business Purpose | Primary Call-to-Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Discover capabilities & verify trust. | Capture inbound freight leads. | **"Request a Freight Quote"** |
| `/about` | Validate corporate credentials. | Establish compliance and governance. | **"Connect with Management"** |
| `/services/*` | Inspect specific cargo modalities. | Educate shipper on SLA & equipment. | **"Get Service Quote"** |
| `/projects` | Review heavy-lift case studies. | Prove technical engineering capability. | **"Consult Project Engineers"** |
| `/locations` | Find nearest branch office. | Enable direct phone/WhatsApp contact. | **"Call Branch Office"** |
| `/quote` | Submit exact cargo parameters. | Generate high-intent sales lead. | **"Submit RFQ"** |
| `/contact` | General / billing / operational inquiries. | Route inquiries to right department. | **"Send Message"** |
