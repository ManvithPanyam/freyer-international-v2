## 4. Analytical Critique: Strongest & Weakest Moments

### Strongest Moment:
**The Sequential Transition from the 482 MT Monument into Ocean & Air Services (`Act II → Act III`).**
The moment the viewer scrolls past the stark 482 MT data block and encounters the architectural bridge (*"THE SAME MARITIME RIGOR. APPLIED TO EVERY CONSIGNMENT."*), the entire website clicks into place. It instantly answers the core skepticism: a company trusted to load 482 tons of breakbulk into a maritime hold will manage containerized or air freight with flawless execution.

### Weakest Moment:
**The Visual Assets for Risk Management.**
While Ocean, Air, Customs, and Warehousing feature authentic documentary photography, Risk Management relies on a conceptual network visualization (`/images/Risk-Management.jpg`). While verified from Freyer's assets, replacing this with an authentic maritime insurance policy binder, marine survey inspection certificate, or cargo surveyor report would further heighten documentary purity.

### Anything Still Needing Polish:
- **Station Head Direct Phone Toggles**: In the India Network, tapping a station updates the address and phone cleanly. Adding an instant WhatsApp/Click-to-Call protocol for mobile users on individual branch lines would further improve operational utility.
- **Port Proximity Tags**: Adding proximity notes to the 10 stations (e.g., Chennai: Port of Chennai / Ennore; Mumbai: Nhava Sheva / JNPT; Tuticorin: VOC Port) would further highlight Freyer's maritime proximity without inventing claims.
