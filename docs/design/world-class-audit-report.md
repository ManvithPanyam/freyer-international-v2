## 2. TOP FIVE GAPS & TOP FIVE STRENGTHS

### Top 5 Strategic Gaps
1. **AI / Stock Imagery in the Hero**: The initial viewport relies on an AI-generated port illustration with a visible disclaimer ("Not a real Freyer facility"), immediately damaging enterprise credibility.
2. **Project Cargo is Still a Card Slider, Not a Displacement Story**: We show photos of cargo, but we don't *demonstrate* the physical engineering reality of moving heavy freight through time and space.
3. **Mobile Map Usability**: The India map is visually clean on a 27" desktop monitor but becomes cramped and frustrating on a 390px iPhone screen.
4. **Energy Drop in Core Services**: After the high-drama Project Cargo section, standard air and ocean services feel like an afterthought, despite representing the bulk of day-to-day corporate revenue.
5. **Passive Conversion Architecture**: Contact information is presented as directory lists rather than an active, fast-response enterprise logistics terminal.

### Top 5 Strengths to Protect
1. **Obsidian & Deep Maritime Palette**: Deep obsidian (`#07152b`), maritime navy (`#0b2144`), and logistics red (`#e1390f`) feel authoritative, serious, and mature.
2. **Absolute Source Truth**: All cargo tonnages, dimensions, and branch locations are 100% verified against official Freyer forensic records (`projects.json` and `locations.json`).
3. **Typographic Scale Contrast**: Bold display numbers (`482 MT`) contrasted against surgical metadata create high-end editorial authority.
4. **Full Geographic Silhouette of India**: Preserving Kashmir, Tamil Nadu, and the peninsular tip sets Freyer apart from careless competitors who use cropped SVGs.
5. **Chennai Egmore Origin Anchor**: Clear, unambiguous visual primacy given to Freyer's founding operational headquarters.

## 6. INTEGRATION ROADMAP: WHAT TO INTEGRATE NEXT VS. WHAT TO NEVER INTEGRATE

### What Should Be Integrated Next
1. **Integrate Experiment 01 (Sovereign Field Hero)**: Replace the current hero on the main homepage to eliminate the AI illustration and establish instant 3-second comprehension.
2. **Integrate Experiment 02 (482 MT Cargo Displacement)**: Upgrade the current Project Cargo section with the 3-phase physical displacement controller.
3. **Integrate Experiment 03 (Adaptive Cartography)**: Replace the static map with the unclipped D3-Geo projection and mobile station drawer sheet.
4. **Integrate Experiment 04 (Services Horizon)**: Unify the 6 core services into a cohesive widescreen capability stage.
5. **Integrate Experiment 05 (Cargo Caliper)**: Feature the Cargo Caliper either within the Project Cargo section or on the dedicated `/project` case studies page as an interactive feasibility showcase.

### What Should ABSOLUTELY NOT Be Integrated
1. **NO AI-generated facilities or illustrations**: Only authentic documentary photography from real operations.
2. **NO synthetic sci-fi calipers or invented HUD terminology**: Only real dimensions and verified permits.
3. **NO heavy WebGL 3D meshes or canvas shaders**: They drain mobile batteries and cause thermal fan spin on corporate laptops.
4. **NO scroll-hijacking or wheel-trapping**: All pinned sequences must have explicit, optional phase buttons so the user is always in control.
5. **NO hidden phone numbers or multi-step quote menus**: Keep direct station contacts accessible in 1 click across every view.
