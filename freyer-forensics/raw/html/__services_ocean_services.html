Logistics helps you realise your business goals with a broad range of transport and logistics services.

Contact Us

We deliver cost effective and efficient solutions by leveraging our long established carrier relationships with years of expertise in ocean freight. With reliable scheduling, global visibility, and a customer first mentality, you can count on Freyer to deliver for you on each and every one of your ocean shipments.

FCL

Our FCL shipping services will help you to secure the capacity and the routing you need at the time you need it. We negotiate competitive agreements with the world’s leading ocean carriers, allowing us to pass the space, service, and pricing benefits of these agreements on to our customers.

Our ocean services teams will provide you with an unparalleled level of support, offering you a consistent and reliable service you can count on to get your full container freight to destination within the required timeframe.

LCL

When your shipments are ready to move but do not fill a full container Freyer has you covered. Our LCL services offer you the ability to move your freight when it is ready in the quickest and most economical manner.

Utilizing Freyer LCL services will help you to reduce inventory levels, give you access to a dependable weekly sailing into and out of all major global locations, provide you with competitive pricing based on the actual volume moved, and grant you with the flexibility you need to operate an efficient and successful supply chain.
